Firefighter Ulam, PHD MSR 9/6/2023 1:35 PM
Just to clarify with regards to the S1 Jump Cancel Trick :
  • The purpose of this is because whenever you cast S1, it moves you a considerable amount of distance. You will then have to waste that 0.5s PER S1 to get back within attacking range, and if you add those 0.5s up you'll easily lose a few free hits of damage.
  • There is no reason to do this if the monster is large in size and hence your S1 will almost never bring you out of range (i.e. perhaps Darkgoo, Seruf), or if you are aiming to hit multiple targets with S1.
