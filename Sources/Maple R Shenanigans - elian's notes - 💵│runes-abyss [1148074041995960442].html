LUCKY RECAST After recasting your runes sufficient time, you will trigger a lucky recast. Lucky Recast allows you to swap one of your existing Bonus Attributes with one of the 4 Random Attribute, where 1 of the Random Attributes is guaranteed to be S rank. Regardless of whether you choose to swap your selected attribute with one of the random attributes, the Lucky Cast will reset afterwards, and will require another 375 Recasts to be triggered again.
