Firefighter Ulam, PHD MSR 9/17/2023 11:23 PM
HOW SHOULD I LEVEL MY MONSTER CARDS? Orange Cards naturally provides more base bonus (Per 5 levels) than Yellow Cards, so you should always embed Orange Cards whenever possible. If the monster cards you possess are low levelled, then it might be better to use Yellow with Set Bonuses instead of changing out to Orange. For instance, if your Orange Monster card will only hit level 10 (at best) then the Yellow Set bonus might have an edge over using that extra Orange card. The levelling category priority is Attack > Guard > Defense. The reason for Guard to be better than Defense is because Guard base stat is Attack % whereas for Defense it's HP Bonus %. The best way to upgrade your cards is to level in sets of 5s, so for each card you'll work on hitting the next 5-level checkpoint before moving on to the next. Each level on your individual monster card will give you 0.1% extra of the base stat bonus that they provide. (Under "Basic Attribute", there's <Base Stat> and <Base Stat Bonus>, I am referring to the bonus here) (edited)
