Star Dream Paradise

Daily task lottery event — complete tasks to earn Star Dream Bears, spend 10 Bears per draw, work through a 30-item prize grid covering Wings, Stigmata, Orbs, Class Cards, and Totems.

Observed Jun 4, 2026 · 27 days remaining Source In-game screenshots · Drive batch IMG_7038–7054 Filed 2026 Read 4 min
⭐

Star Dream Paradise is a daily-task lottery event lasting approximately 28 days. The core loop is simple: complete daily tasks to collect Star Dream Bears, spend 10 Bears per draw, and work through a 30-slot jackpot grid until each item reaches its obtainment limit and is removed. The prize pool covers a wide range of upgrade materials — making this worth playing daily regardless of which upgrade system you are currently pushing.

Event window Screenshots captured Jun 4, 2026 with ~27 days remaining. The event resets daily at 06:00. Prize table and task types are documented from observed screenshots — exact quantities visible in the full prize table below.

Jackpot grid rules

The grid contains 30 items. Each slot has a flat 3.33% probability per draw. When an item reaches its maximum obtainment limit it is permanently removed from the grid, improving the odds for remaining items.

  • Most items have a limit of 2 — once you obtain the same item twice, it is removed.
  • Scarce items (Wing Fragments, Orb Fragment, Pet Fragment) appear to be limited at 1.
  • If you hold unclaimed cumulative milestones when the event ends, they are reissued by mail.
  • Unused Star Dream Bears are recovered (cleared) when the event ends — spend them before it closes.

§ 05

Daily priority

Maximising Bears per day

Free-task floor

Nine of the eleven observed task types are free — they require only routine daily activity (Grand Arena check-in, World Boss challenge, Guild Sign-in, Quick Battle, etc.). Completing all free tasks before reset gives a reliable floor of approximately 9 Bears per day without spending anything.

At 10 Bears per draw, a full free run over ~28 days yields approximately 25 draws minimum, covering most of the jackpot grid once. The "Top up any amount" and "Consume 100 diamonds" tasks add 1–2 Bears each for players who spend, but skipping them does not significantly change your total grid coverage.

Don't hold Bears Unused Bears are cleared when the event ends, not returned. Spend Bears as you accumulate them — there is no advantage to holding them, and the jackpot odds improve as items leave the grid, so earlier draws face worse odds than later draws.
