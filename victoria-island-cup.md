# Victoria Island Cup — Complete Event Guide
*Maple R Evolution | 2026 FIFA World Cup Crossover Event*

---

## Overview

The **Victoria Island Cup** is a limited-time crossover event tied to the real **2026 FIFA World Cup**. Lore-wise, as the Black Mage's forces are temporarily suppressed, a hyper-dimensional wormhole opens on Victoria Island. A football flies through from a strange world — signalling the start of the annual Victoria Island Cup. Warriors must heed Empress Cygnus's call and bring glory back to Maple Island.

The event has four tabs: **Turf Challenge**, **Prediction**, **Team Cheer**, and **World Shop**.

The event runs for approximately **41 days** total.

## TAB 1: Turf Challenge

### Background
A training camp established by Empress Cygnus to prepare for the Victoria Island Cup. It is rumoured that the warrior who stands until the final challenge will be crowned the Football King of Maple Island.

### How to Play
Turf Challenge is a **shooting mini-game**. Each attempt has two phases:

1. **Direction phase** — Hold to adjust the shot direction and power, then release to confirm.
2. **Power phase** — A vertical power gauge (red/yellow/green/blue bar) on the right side indicates power level. Release at the right moment.

The goalkeeper adjusts on their own during your setup. Once your adjustments are complete, the keeper will attempt to save. If the keeper saves, the current shot fails. The challenge ends when all attempts are exhausted.

- Each game has a fixed number of **Remaining Attempts** (e.g. 10 attempts).
- You must score a target number of goals **in a single game** to complete a tier.
- **Goal rewards must be manually claimed** — they are not distributed automatically.

### Stage Unlock
- There are **41 difficulty stages** in total.
- Stages 1–3 are available immediately. Stages 4–41 unlock on a fixed **real-world day schedule**.
- You must **complete all goal objectives of the current stage** before accessing the next difficulty tier.
- Returning to the main interface after clearing a stage automatically advances you to the next difficulty.

### Difficulty Phases Summary
The modifiers shift at clear breakpoints:
- **Diff 1–2:** Standard (no modifiers)
- **Diff 3–11:** Goalkeeper Speed increases 10% per stage (110% → 190%)
- **Diff 12–16:** Goalkeeper Speed continues (200%–240%) + Defensive Range added at 150%
- **Diff 17–31:** Control system becomes harder (Direction +150%, Power +150%); goal targets lower
- **Diff 32–41:** Maximum difficulty (Direction +200%, Power +200%); goal targets drop to 1/2/3

## TAB 2: Prediction

### Background
The Prediction tab centres on the **2026 International Football Tournament**. Players bet Prediction Coins on real match outcomes to earn Prediction Points and climb the leaderboard.

### Core Mechanics

- Players must **consume Prediction Coins** to place a prediction on a match.
- **Correct prediction:** Prediction Coins are refunded + you earn **Prediction Points** equal to the coins wagered.
- **Wrong prediction:** Coins are lost.
- Predictions **lock 30 minutes before kickoff**.
- Match results update daily at **13:00 and 19:00**. Rewards for successful predictions are distributed at these two times.

### Prediction Coins (Recap)
- Earned from match predictions (correct ones), Daily Tasks, and Prediction Packs.
- Usable in both the Prediction tab (betting) and the World Shop.
- Not only for match predictions — also exchangeable for rare items in the World Shop.
- Coins are retrieved when the event ends (returned to you), so don't hoard them too long.

### Prediction Ranking
- Prediction Points = the amount of Prediction Coins used on **correct** predictions.
- Points determine your rank on the **Prediction Ranking List**.
- Ranking rewards are mailed after the event ends.

## TAB 3: Team Cheer

### What Is It

During the event, players cheer for their favourite teams from the 2026 World Cup by purchasing **Cheer Packs**. Purchasing a pack grants **Cheer Points** to that team, which determines team popularity ranking within your server group.

Even if a team is eliminated from the real-world 2026 World Cup, they can still rank as the most popular team in your server group.

### Cheer Pack Rules

- Cheer Packs can be purchased for **any team**.
- Each team has **2 pack types**: Basic Cheer Pack and Advanced Cheer Pack.
- If your team wins the actual 2026 World Cup, you receive a **Championship Reward** at the end of the event.
- **Eliminated teams grant no Championship Reward** — only the popularity rank remains.
- **The earlier you buy, the better the Championship Reward.** Buying during Group Stage > Round of 32 > later rounds.
- Maximum of **2 Championship Rewards per team** (one per pack type).

## Tips and Strategy

**1. Complete every Daily Task**
Daily Tasks are your most efficient coin and Golden Football generator. They progress both Prediction Challenge Tasks and the Turf Benefit track simultaneously. Missing days compounds quickly over a 41-day event.

**2. Claim Successful Prediction rewards manually**
This is the single most commonly missed mechanic. The "Successfully predict X times" Challenge Task requires you to **manually open each correct prediction's result and claim it**. Auto-distribution does not register toward quest progress.

**3. Back your team early**
If you're confident in a team's World Cup run, buy their Cheer Pack during the Group Stage. A Group Stage purchase yields a better Championship Reward than buying at the same team during knockouts. Each team has two pack types, so you can potentially earn two Championship Rewards per winning team.

**4. Bet big on high-confidence matches**
Prediction ranking is driven purely by coins used on *correct* predictions. Small bets on easy picks don't move the needle as much as large bets on confident ones. Identify the most predictable matchups (strong vs. weak groups) and put maximum coins there.

**5. Spend Prediction Coins before they expire**
Unspent coins are retrieved at event end, but only temporarily — plan your World Shop purchases. If you can't spend them on predictions, use them on HP Potential Stones (60 coins each, 100/week) or Coin purchases (no limit) rather than let them sit.

**6. Turf Challenge pacing**
Difficulties 4–41 unlock on a fixed day schedule regardless of your personal progress. Always clear your current max stage before the next one opens so you never fall behind. There's no penalty for playing lower difficulties again, but you can only progress the reward by meeting the goal targets.

**7. Direction + Power Control at high difficulties**
From Diff 17 onward, both your aim accuracy and power timing become significantly less forgiving. Treat these stages as genuine reflex tests — the goal targets are reduced (3/5/8 down to 1/2/3) to compensate, so aim for the lower targets first to guarantee at least some reward before attempting the harder tiers.

**8. Advanced Cheer Pack value proposition**
At 500 Diamonds, the team jersey (ATK 700, HP 13,500, DEF 0.3%) is a permanent stat costume with respectable numbers. If you're planning to spend Diamonds on the event at all, this is the highest-value cosmetic item available.

**9. Turf Benefit — don't sleep on the Basic track**
The free Basic track includes the **Victoria Island Training** avatar frame (Permanent) and other materials. Even without purchasing the paid tiers, you can complete most of the Basic track by doing daily tasks consistently. The Advanced track's "Turf Passion" chat frame and "Football" avatar are both permanent cosmetics worth considering if you want a memento from the event.

**10. World Shop weekly reset**
The shop resets weekly. If you have surplus coins, prioritise the low-weekly-limit items (ATK Potential Stone: 10/week, Divine Moon Stone: 20/week) before they reset, and cycle through multiple weeks if your coin income supports it.
