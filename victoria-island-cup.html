Victoria Island Cup

2026 FIFA World Cup crossover event — soccer shooting mini-game with 41 difficulty stages, Group Stage match predictions for all 48 nations, Team Cheer pack mechanics, and a full World Shop reference.

Observed Jun 10, 2026 · 17 days remaining Source In-game screenshots · Drive batch IMG_7196–7333 Filed 2026 Read 10 min
⚽

The Victoria Island Cup is a ~41-day limited event tied to the real 2026 FIFA World Cup. Lore-wise, a hyper-dimensional wormhole opens on Victoria Island as the Black Mage's forces subside — a football flies through and Empress Cygnus calls all warriors to compete. In practice, four independent systems run simultaneously: a soccer shooting mini-game (Turf Challenge), a prediction market on real World Cup matches, a team popularity contest (Team Cheer), and an event-exclusive shop.

Event window Screenshots captured Jun 10, 2026 with ~17 days remaining — event started approximately Jun 1 and runs ~41 days total. Difficulty unlock timers, match schedule, and shop prices are verified from in-game UI. All times listed are in-game server time.
§ 01

Event currencies

Two things to track

Prediction Coins

The primary event currency. Earned from correct match predictions, completing Daily Tasks in the Prediction tab, and Prediction Packs. Used for placing match bets, buying Basic Cheer Packs, and shopping in the World Shop.

⚠️ Prediction Coins are event-exclusive. When the event ends they are returned to you temporarily — spend them in the World Shop before the timer expires.

Golden Football

A secondary tracker earned exclusively by completing Daily Tasks in the Prediction tab. Does not serve as a currency — it fills the Turf Benefit reward track instead. The more you earn, the further you progress along the benefit milestones.

§ 02

Turf Challenge

Shooting mini-game · 41 stages

Turf Challenge is a soccer shooting mini-game. Score the required number of goals within limited attempts to clear each stage's reward tiers. The keeper gets harder as stages unlock over the event's 41-day schedule.

How to play

Each shot has two alternating phases. The phases alternate until all attempts are used or you exit.

  • Direction phase — Hold the on-screen button to adjust shot direction and power, release to confirm.
  • Power phase — A vertical bar (red → yellow → green → blue) shows power level. Release at the desired level.
  • The goalkeeper adjusts on their own during setup. Once your adjustments are complete, the keeper attempts the save. If they succeed: Shot Failed. If not: goal scored.
  • Each difficulty has a fixed number of Remaining Attempts (starts at 10). You must reach the goal target in a single game session.

Stage unlock rules

  • Stages 1–3 are available from day one. Stages 4–41 unlock on a fixed daily timer.
  • You must complete all three goal tiers of the current stage before the next difficulty becomes accessible.
  • Goal rewards are not automatic — tap the reward button to claim each tier manually.
  • Returning to the Turf Challenge main screen after clearing a stage auto-advances your selection to the next difficulty.
Each difficulty has 3 goal tiers. The third tier (max goals) on some stages rewards a rare item instead of standard coins — watch for the distinct icon.

§ 03

Check-In & Benefit

Daily login · Cumulative track

Turf Check-In

A standard 41-day login calendar. Log in each day to claim the reward — no additional action required. Rewards rotate across Prediction Coins, Red Gems, Triangle crafting materials, and cosmetic items on milestone days (approximately every 5–6 days).

Turf Benefit

A cumulative reward track filled by collecting Golden Footballs, which are earned by completing Daily Tasks in the Prediction tab. Three parallel reward columns exist:

  • Basic Benefits (free) — Includes the Victoria Island Training avatar frame (Permanent), Moon Stones, Red Gems, Triangle items, and Prediction Coins. Available to everyone.
  • Intermediate Benefits (5 Hira) — Additional rewards unlocked at the same progress thresholds. Includes Red Gems, Triangle items, Soccer Ball fragments, Magic Solvents, Moon Stones.
  • Advanced Benefits (8 Hira) — Includes the Turf Passion chat frame (Permanent) and the Football avatar (Permanent), plus Red Gems, Moon Stones, and Coins.

Milestones run from ~50 Golden Footballs up to 3,000+. Unclaimed rewards when the event ends are sent by mail.

Free floor The Basic track gives a permanent avatar frame at no cost. If you complete Daily Tasks consistently, you will reach it without spending anything. Intermediate and Advanced packs are optional cosmetic upgrades.
§ 04

Match Prediction

Bet coins · Earn points · Climb ranks

Core mechanic

  • Consume Prediction Coins to place a bet on a match outcome (win / draw / loss).
  • Correct prediction: Coins are fully refunded + you earn Prediction Points equal to the amount wagered.
  • Wrong prediction: Coins are lost.
  • Predictions lock 30 minutes before kickoff.
  • Results update daily at 13:00 and 19:00. Rewards for successful predictions are distributed at these two times only.

Prediction Ranking

Prediction Points = the amount of Prediction Coins used on correct predictions only. Points determine your rank on the server leaderboard. Ranking rewards are mailed after the event ends.

  • Guild Sign-in (1×)

  • Complete Adventurer Training Field (1×)

  • Like any player (1×)

  • Challenge World Boss (1×)
  • Message in any chat channel (1×)
  • Consume 100 red or blue diamonds

Challenge Tasks (one-time milestones · reward Prediction Coins)

Two milestone ladders exist:

  • Make a prediction X times — milestones at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 … 64 total predictions (even increments). Each rewards a stack of Prediction Coins (increasing at higher milestones).
  • Successfully predict X times — milestones at 1 through 24 successful predictions. Each rewards a stack of Prediction Coins.
Manual claim required The "Successfully predict X times" milestones will not advance unless you manually open each correct prediction result and claim it. Auto-distribution at 13:00/19:00 does not count toward quest completion. Check the Prediction tab after each results window.

§ 06

Team Cheer

Back a team · Win Championship Rewards

How it works

  • Buy a Cheer Pack for any of the 48 teams. This grants Cheer Points to that team, contributing to the server popularity ranking.
  • If your chosen team wins the 2026 World Cup, you receive a Championship Reward mailed at event end.
  • Eliminated teams grant no Championship Reward — but still accumulate popularity points and can win the server popularity contest.
  • Earlier purchase = better Championship Reward. Group Stage purchase beats a Round of 32 purchase, which beats later rounds.
  • Each team has 2 pack types, so a single winning team can yield up to 2 Championship Rewards.

§ 08

Daily priority

What to do each session

Non-negotiables

  • Complete all Daily Tasks — they award both Prediction Coins and Golden Footballs in the same action. Skipping compounds daily.
  • Claim Turf Check-In — one tap, no action required.
  • Place predictions before kickoff — predictions lock 30 min before each match. Missed matches are coins left on the table.
  • Manually claim successful prediction rewards — results appear at 13:00 and 19:00. Open each correct result and claim it. Without this, the "Successfully predict X times" Challenge Task will not advance.
  • Clear current Turf Challenge stage — always clear your highest unlocked difficulty before the next one opens. Falling behind means double work.

Weekly reset actions

  • Buy ATK Potential Stones (10 weekly cap — prioritise first).
  • Buy Divine Moon Stones (20 weekly cap, 100 coins each — strong value).
  • Buy remaining high-priority items (DEF Stones, Sewing Wand) in the World Shop.

Before the event ends

  • Spend all remaining Prediction Coins in the World Shop — Coin purchases (30 coins, no limit) absorb any leftover balance.
  • Back a team with a Basic Cheer Pack if you believe they will win and haven't yet — deadline is whenever that team is eliminated.
  • Claim all unclaimed Turf Challenge rewards manually before the timer hits zero.
Ranking strategy Prediction Points = coins wagered on correct predictions only. The ranking is won by large correct bets, not by volume of small ones. Identify the most predictable matchups — dominant teams in weak groups — and concentrate your maximum coin bet there.
